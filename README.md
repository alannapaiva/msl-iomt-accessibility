# Systematic mapping data: accessibility in IoMT interfaces

Supplementary material for the article **"Accessibility Features in Internet of Medical Things Interfaces: Impacts on User Experience"**, by Alanna Maria Machado Alves Paiva, Tadeu dos Santos Jerônimo, Evilásio C. Junior, Rhuan da Silva Nunes and José Cláudio do Nascimento (Federal University of Ceará, Brazil).

The mapping followed the PRISMA 2020 guidelines and covered publications from January 2020 to September 2026 in six databases (Scopus, EI Compendex, Web of Science, ScienceDirect, ACM Digital Library and IEEE Xplore). Of the 460 records identified, 21 primary studies with evaluation by real users were selected.

## Contents

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| [`PROTOCOL.md`](PROTOCOL.md) | Mapping protocol: objective, research questions (RQ1–RQ5), inclusion and exclusion criteria, search strings, study selection, extraction fields and quality assessment questions. |
| `data/01_screening.xlsx` | Eligibility decision for each assessed record, with the criterion applied and the justification. **Screening** sheet: records accepted at title and abstract screening in Parsifal and all IEEE Xplore records (export and results pages not included in the export). **PRISMA summary** sheet: numbers for each stage of the flow diagram. **Criteria** sheet: inclusion and exclusion criteria and interpretation rules. |
| `data/02_data_extraction.xlsx` | Completed extraction form for the 21 studies (fields DE-1 to DE-14, context of use and comparator), the verbatim excerpts supporting fields DE-5, DE-7, DE-8 and DE-9, and the description of each form field. |
| `data/03_quality_assessment.xlsx` | Score of each study on questions QA-1 to QA-5 (0; 0.5; 1) and total score (maximum 5). |
| `data/*.csv` | CSV versions (UTF-8) of the main tables, for use without Excel. |

The included studies are identified by codes **E01 to E21**, the same codes used in the article. The "Source ID" column (ART_xxx for Parsifal records, IEEE #n for the IEEE Xplore export and IEEE-PDF-nn for records from other sources) links each record to its origin in the screening.

Codes used in the article and in these files: **RQ** = research question, **IC** = inclusion criterion, **EC** = exclusion criterion, **DE** = data extraction field, **QA** = quality assessment question.

## Notes

- Title and abstract screening of the records from the first five databases was performed on the Parsifal platform; the screening file contains the full-text eligibility decisions for those records and all decisions for IEEE Xplore records.
- The full texts of the analyzed studies are not provided because they are protected by copyright; complete references are listed in the article.
- Eligibility checking and data extraction were supported by language models to locate excerpts in the articles; decisions and values were recorded with a justification and an evidence excerpt and reviewed by the authors.
